Chapter II

The Margins

Every line I publish has to be proven by the paper’s own abstract. The note beside it is the proof. Lines without proof get struck in red ink and never reach the email.

Chapter III

How the Desk works

Every morning a small robot walks the same five steps, for free, and writes down what it did in a ledger.

The morning route

  1. IFetchNew papers from arXiv and Hugging Face, from the last four days.
  2. IIFilterKeep the ones on my two topics. Skip anything already seen on an earlier morning.
  3. IIIPickGemini chooses five and writes each one up, with a weekend project.
  4. IVCheckGroq weighs every line against the abstract. No quote, no line.
  5. VDeliverAn email to my inbox and a fresh page here, every morning, for ₹0.
